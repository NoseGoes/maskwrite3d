Image Segmentation Studio for iPad and iPhone

Segment 3D images
anywhere you are.

Maskwrite 3D is a desktop-class segmentation editor that fits in your hand. Open a CT, MRI, microscopy stack or any 3D image, paint masks with Apple Pencil or a finger, fill the gaps between slices automatically, and export straight to 3D Slicer, your 3D printer or your training pipeline.

What you can do

Paint with precision

Paint and erase in linked axial, coronal and sagittal views. Apple Pencil edits while fingers scroll, pan and zoom, or turn on finger drawing on any device.

Fill between slices

Draw a few slices and shape-based interpolation fills the rest, following the image so masks land on real edges. Review and spot-fix anywhere.

Threshold and grow

Pick an intensity range to capture bone, air or contrast in one step, limit it to a box, or grow a region from seeds and keep edits inside the range you choose.

Clean up fast

Lasso and split regions, remove islands and specks, smooth without losing volume, and dilate or erode by millimetres. Undo and redo everything.

See it in 3D

Generate a smoothed surface of any mask, or render the CT itself as a volume with bone and soft tissue presets and cut planes you can move.

Export anywhere

3D Slicer segmentations with names and colors, NIfTI labels, STL models, the source image and volume statistics, all saved into Files.

Open what you have

NIfTI, uncompressed DICOM series, NRRD, 3D Slicer segmentations, TIFF stacks and folders of images. Slightly tilted scans keep their exact geometry in exports, and coronal or sagittal acquisitions are reordered for you.

Processing stays on your device. Nothing is uploaded by the app.

Your work, your files

Projects autosave and reopen where you left off. Exports land in Maskwrite 3D › Exports in the Files app, ready to AirDrop, share or sync with any cloud provider you choose.

Mask names and colors travel with your exports, so what you see on the iPad is what opens on the desktop.

Free to start. Pro when you need it.

Free includes opening your volumes, viewing, painting, interpolation, one mask per volume, undo and project backups. Pro is a one-time purchase, no subscription, with the price shown in the app.

Pro unlocks additional masks, threshold segmentation, split, labelmap import and every export: 3D Slicer, NIfTI, STL and CSV. Existing projects and masks stay intact if Pro is ever unavailable.

Supported input

Scalar 3D volumes: NIfTI-1 (.nii, .nii.gz), uncompressed single-frame DICOM series, NRRD and .seg.nrrd, TIFF stacks (uncompressed, LZW, Deflate, PackBits) and image folders. Compressed or multi-frame DICOM, BigTIFF and scans tilted more than two degrees are not supported yet.
