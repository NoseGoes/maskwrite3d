Privacy policy

Your images stay in your workflow.

Effective October 31, 2026

Image processing and local storage

Maskwrite 3D processes volumes and segmentation masks on your device. The app has no developer-operated image upload service, advertising, analytics SDK, tracking SDK or developer-operated crash-reporting service. Your imported images, masks, projects and editing history are not sent to us by the app.

The app stores recovery projects and editing preferences locally. It accesses file metadata to open selected documents and manage projects, not to identify or track users.

Files, cloud providers and backups

Exports are written to the app's folder in Apple's Files app, and you choose where to share or move them. iCloud Drive, Google Drive or another provider may store or synchronize those files according to its own privacy policy and your settings. Apple device and iCloud backups may include app data. Local processing does not mean that files you choose to share, sync or back up remain only on your device.

Purchases and Apple services

Pro is an optional one-time purchase processed by Apple. The app uses Apple-verified transaction information to check access to Pro and restore purchases. We do not receive your payment-card details. Apple may process purchases, store analytics, TestFlight feedback and platform diagnostics under Apple's own policies. Volume and mask data is not required to purchase or restore Pro.

Support

If you email MaskWrite3D@outlook.com, we and our email provider receive the information you voluntarily send and use it to respond to your request. Please avoid sending identifiable scans or other sensitive material. You may contact this address with privacy questions or requests about support correspondence.

Managing your files

You control exported copies in Files and your selected cloud providers. Deleting the app removes app-local data subject to Apple's platform behavior. Exported copies, synchronized files and backups must be managed separately with their providers.

This website

This site does not use analytics scripts, advertising trackers or account registration. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, whose provider may process technical request information needed to deliver and secure the site. Email links open your chosen email app; contacting support is voluntary.

Updates

Changes to this policy will appear here with an updated effective date. Contact MaskWrite3D@outlook.com with questions.
