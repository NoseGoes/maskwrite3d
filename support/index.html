Your first mask

  1. Start with the included sample volume, or tap Open and choose a NIfTI, DICOM, NRRD or TIFF file, or a folder of images. For DICOM, pick the series you want.
  2. Tap Create Mask, choose Paint and draw with Apple Pencil. Turn on Finger drawing to paint with a finger. On iPhone, finger drawing is always on; hold Paint or Erase to change the brush size.
  3. With finger drawing off, one finger scrolls slices. Two fingers pan and a pinch zooms. The − and + buttons beside each slider step one slice; hold to keep going.
  4. Paint another slice in the same plane. Auto fill interpolates the gap. Review every result and fix it with Erase or Lasso.
  5. Tap Generate 3D for a surface preview, or switch the 3D view to CT to render the volume with cut planes.

Window and level

Tap W / L, then drag on any slice: left and right change the window, up and down change the level. Double tap resets, and Presets offers bone, soft tissue and air. Tap Done to go back to editing.

Threshold, grow and cleanup

Threshold previews an intensity range live. Narrow it to a box with the axis sliders, then apply it to the active mask. Grow expands from the points you tap within the range. Islands keeps the largest pieces or removes specks. Morphology smooths without changing volume, dilates or erodes by millimetres and removes specks. Keep edits inside this range stops the brush from leaving the intensity range you set.

Save and export

Projects save automatically on your device. Save › Save project copy writes a .maskwrite file you can keep anywhere and reopen with Open.

Everything under Export is saved into Files › On My iPad › Maskwrite 3D › Exports and a Share button appears. Nothing is overwritten; repeated exports get a number.

Exports require Pro. Keep the exported source image alongside your masks so they always line up.

Restore Pro

Open Pro and tap Restore Purchases with the Apple Account that bought it. Apple may ask you to sign in. If products are unavailable, check your connection and try again. Your masks and projects are never affected.

Large volumes and memory

Volumes up to 160 million voxels and 768 MiB uncompressed are accepted. Big scans work best on recent iPads; if the app runs out of memory, try a lower 3D detail setting, Clear preview, or crop the scan before importing.

Deleting a mask asks for confirmation, removes its voxels and clears its undo history. Unlock a locked mask before deleting it.

Format limits

NIfTI types uint8, int16, uint16, int32 and float32. DICOM must be uncompressed and single-frame; decompress other series first, for example by exporting from 3D Slicer. TIFF supports no compression, LZW, Deflate and PackBits, not BigTIFF. Scans tilted more than two degrees from the axes are refused. Labels are exclusive, not overlapping layers.

AI segmentation is not included. Support for running your own trained models is planned.
